Autoonn - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Autoonn handles information when users connect their accounts and use Autoonn's content management and publishing features.

1. Information We May Process

When a user connects a TikTok account, Autoonn may receive information made available through the official TikTok APIs and authorized by the user. This may include basic profile information, account identifiers, authorization information, and information necessary to manage or publish content.

2. How We Use Information

Information obtained through authorized integrations is used only to provide Autoonn features requested by the user, such as connecting an account, managing content, and submitting user-selected videos for publication.

3. TikTok Integration

Autoonn uses official TikTok APIs. Access to TikTok information and features is subject to the permissions explicitly authorized by the user and to TikTok's applicable policies.

Autoonn does not request or store users' TikTok passwords.

4. Access Tokens

Authentication credentials or access tokens may be processed when necessary to communicate securely with authorized third-party APIs. Such credentials are used only for authorized application functions and should be protected from unauthorized access.

5. Content

Videos, captions, and other content selected by users may be processed as necessary to provide content management or publishing functionality. Users retain responsibility for and ownership of their content, subject to the terms of the platforms where it is published.

6. Sharing of Information

Autoonn does not sell users' personal information. Information may be transmitted to third-party platforms when necessary to perform actions specifically requested and authorized by the user.

7. Data Retention and Security

Autoonn seeks to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its services, meet security requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations. Reasonable measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

8. User Choices and Account Access

Users may revoke Autoonn's access to connected third-party accounts using the authorization or account-management options provided by those platforms.

9. Children's Privacy

Autoonn is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws or platform requirements.

10. Third-Party Services

Autoonn may interact with third-party services such as TikTok. Those services operate according to their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes to Autoonn, legal requirements, security practices, or third-party platform requirements. The current version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the official Autoonn support contact.
